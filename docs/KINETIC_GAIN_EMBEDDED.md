# Kinetic Gain Embedded

Revenue Control Breakage Map is designed to open an embedded executive-intelligence engagement:

- expose where attribution drift, reporting fragmentation, and control gaps are eroding board confidence
- identify which lanes need reconciliation, standardization, instrumentation, escalation, or containment before the next review cycle
- convert a broad revenue story into one usable breakage packet with explicit owners and recovery decisions

This is an operator-facing executive-intelligence surface, not accounting advice, legal advice, financial advice, or a live market system.
